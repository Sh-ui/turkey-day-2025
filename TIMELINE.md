# Turkey Day 2025 - Comprehensive Timeline

## Target: Eat Friday, November 28th at 2:00 PM

---

## **Sunday, November 23rd (TODAY)**

### Evening - Start Thawing Turkey
- **Action**: Place frozen turkey (13 lbs) in refrigerator to thaw
- **Location**: Bottom shelf of fridge on a tray to catch drips
- **Note**: 13 lb turkey needs ~3-4 days to thaw; starting now gives you just enough time

---

## **Sunday, November 23rd → Monday, November 24th at Midnight**

### Midnight - Start Shio-Koji Sous-Vide Acceleration
- **Action**: Mix your shio-koji (koji rice, water, salt - Noma ratio if using)
- **Setup**: Place jar in sous-vide bath set to 91°F (32°C)
- **Container**: Use loose lid or breathable cover (cling film with pinholes)
- **Duration**: Will run for 72 hours at 91°F

---

## **Monday, November 24th**

### Throughout Day
- **Shio-koji**: Continues in 91°F sous-vide bath
- **Turkey**: Continues thawing in fridge
- **Optional**: Stir shio-koji once during the day

---

## **Tuesday, November 25th**

### Throughout Day
- **Shio-koji**: Final day in 91°F sous-vide bath (completes 72 hours around midnight)
- **Turkey**: Continue thawing; check if fully thawed by evening

---

## **Tuesday, November 25th → Wednesday, November 26th at Midnight**

### Midnight - Butcher Turkey & Start Koji Marinade

**Shio-Koji Prep:**
- Remove shio-koji from sous-vide bath (72 hours at 91°F complete)
- Blend until smooth, pancake-batter consistency
- Optional: dilute slightly with water for better coverage
- Let cool to room temperature if still warm

**Turkey Butchering:**
- Confirm turkey is fully thawed (flex the joints to check)
- Break down turkey: separate breasts, legs, wings, backbone
- Reserve carcass, wings, neck, giblets for stock

**Koji Marinade Application:**
- Use approximately 2-3 tablespoons shio-koji per pound of meat
- Coat turkey breasts and legs thoroughly with blended shio-koji
- Vacuum seal each piece individually (one breast per bag, one leg per bag)
- Place vacuum-sealed bags in refrigerator
- **Duration**: Turkey will marinade for 36 hours

---

## **Wednesday, November 26th**

### Throughout Day
- **Turkey**: Continues marinating in koji in refrigerator (36 hours total)
- **Shio-koji**: Remaining portion sits at room temperature to mellow (optional; you only used some for the turkey)

### Evening - Prep Check
- Verify you have all ingredients for stock tomorrow
- Turkey remains in koji marinade overnight

---

## **Wednesday, November 26th → Thursday, November 27th at Midnight**

### Midnight - Remove Turkey from Koji & Start Drying

**Remove from Marinade:**
- Take all vacuum-sealed turkey pieces out of refrigerator
- Open bags and remove turkey parts
- Pat off excess koji paste thoroughly (or gently rinse under cold water, then pat completely dry)
- Wipe/scrape surface to remove residual koji (prevents burning during roasting)

**Start Drying Phase:**
- Place turkey pieces on wire rack set over sheet pan
- Leave uncovered in refrigerator
- Maximize air circulation around each piece
- **Duration**: Turkey will air-dry for 36 hours to develop crispy skin

---

## **Thursday, November 27th - Thanksgiving Day**

### Afternoon/Evening - Make Turkey Stock

**Stock Preparation:**
- Use reserved turkey parts (backbone, wings, neck, giblets, carcass pieces)
- Add aromatics: onion, celery, carrot, thyme, bay leaves, peppercorns
- Simmer 3-4 hours, strain
- Cool stock quickly (ice bath, shallow containers)
- Refrigerate overnight

**Turkey Status:**
- Continues air-drying in refrigerator uncovered

---

## **Friday, November 28th - Turkey Day!**

### 10:00 AM - Final Prep
- Remove turkey from refrigerator 
- Let sit at room temperature for 30-60 minutes (optional, helps even cooking)
- Prepare roasting setup: wire rack on sheet pan, small amount of water in bottom
- Set up combustion/predictive thermometer

### 12:00 PM (Noon) - Turkey Goes in Oven

**Roasting Strategy:**
- Start with turkey legs on rack in oven
- Target internal temp: 185°F for legs (dark meat, long cook)
- Use predictive thermometer to calculate timing

**Breast Timing:**
- Based on leg predictions, add breasts to oven
- Time it so breasts finish around 155°F when legs hit 185°F
- Both should finish around same time

### ~1:30 PM - Rest & Make Gravy
- Remove turkey pieces to rest (off rack, tent with foil if needed)
- Move roasting pan to stovetop
- Deglaze pan, add turkey stock from Thursday
- Build gravy with roux + potato starch + gelatin + xanthan (per your gravy technique)
- Season to taste

### 2:00 PM - **EAT!** 🦃
