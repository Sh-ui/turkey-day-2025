# Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, Nov 27) - Japanese-American Fall Fusion

**Concept**: Light, warming Japanese-style home cooking meets American autumn flavors while the turkey stock simmers.

---

## Main: Dashi-Boosted Turkey Stock Soba Bowl

A comforting noodle soup made from unfinished turkey stock enriched with dashi.

**Components:**
- Turkey stock (in-progress, from simmering carcass)
- Dashi powder or concentrate
- Soba noodles
- Frozen peas
- Mushrooms (shiitake, enoki, or cremini)
- Green onions (sliced)
- Optional: soft-boiled egg, crispy turkey skin crumbles

**Method:**
1. While main turkey stock pot simmers, ladle some unfinished stock into a second pot
2. Boost with dashi to taste
3. Add sliced mushrooms, simmer until tender
4. Cook soba noodles separately, drain
5. Add frozen peas to broth (last minute)
6. Assemble: noodles in bowl, ladle hot broth with mushrooms and peas
7. Top with sliced green onions and optional garnishes

---

## Side 1: Fall-Spiced Edamame

Simple steamed edamame tossed in Japanese-American fall fusion seasonings.

**Flavor options to explore:**
- Toasted sesame oil + maple syrup + soy sauce + chili flakes
- Miso butter + brown sugar + black pepper
- Furikake + smoked paprika
- Garlic-ginger butter + cinnamon (subtle)

**Method:**
1. Steam or boil frozen edamame in pods
2. Drain well
3. Toss with chosen flavor combination while hot
4. Serve warm

---

## Side 2: Mixed Greens Salad with Japanese Dressing

Fresh, crisp contrast to the warm noodle bowl.

**Components:**
- Bag of mixed American greens (already have: FE SPRING MIX BABY)
- Japanese dressing (ginger-carrot, sesame-miso, or wafu)
- Coarse black pepper
- Crispy turkey skin from roasted wings (optional topping)

**Turkey Skin Prep:**
1. After roasting turkey scraps but before adding to stock pot, remove and save wing skin
2. Press between paper towels to remove excess fat
3. Roast separately until crispy and golden
4. Break into bite-sized pieces
5. Use as a savory, umami-rich "crouton" alternative

**Assembly:**
- Toss greens lightly with Japanese dressing
- Top with black pepper
- Garnish with crispy turkey skin pieces

## Timeline Integration

**Thursday, November 27th (Thanksgiving Day)**

- **Morning**: Start turkey stock (3-4 hours simmer)
- **Early prep**: Roast turkey wing scraps, reserve crispy skin before adding bones to stock
- **Lunch/Dinner time**: 
  - Ladle some stock into second pot
  - Boost with dashi
  - Cook soba, prep vegetables
  - Steam edamame, toss with fusion spices
  - Dress salad, top with turkey skin
  - Serve comforting Japanese-American fusion meal

**Evening**: Continue stock simmer, cool, refrigerate for Turkey Day (Friday) gravy

## Notes

- This meal is intentionally light and quick since you'll be prepping turkey for roasting the next day
- Uses in-progress stock as a flavor base without depleting your gravy supply
- Bridges the Thanksgiving → Turkey Day experience with fusion flavors
- Leftovers can be easily reheated Friday morning if you want a pre-feast snack
