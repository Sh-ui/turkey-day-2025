# Leftovers Pie

## The Philosophy

Why pull out five different containers, build a plate, and microwave it into a sad pile of separated leftovers when you can turn everything into one cohesive dish? Leftovers Pie takes all your Turkey Day components and layers them into a single, easily stored, easily reheated meal. One container in the fridge. One scoop to serve. One reheat in the oven or microwave. The layers meld together as they sit, the gravy soaks into everything, and you get a complete Thanksgiving in every bite. Serve it with a spoonful of that homemade cranberry orange compote on the side.

---

## The Structure

**Bottom Layer: Stuffing**
- Acts as the base and absorbs gravy
- Provides structure so the pie holds together when serving
- Gets even better as it soaks up flavors from above

**Middle Layers: Turkey + Sides + Gravy**
- Shredded or chopped koji turkey (dark and white meat mixed)
- Brussels sprouts with sausage
- Any other sides (carrots, green beans, whatever you made)
- Pour gravy generously over everything—this is the binder

**Top Layer: Hasselback Potatoes Au Gratin**
- The crispy, creamy top becomes the "crust"
- Creates a golden, bubbly surface when reheated
- Protects everything underneath from drying out

---

## Assembly

1. **Choose your vessel**: Deep casserole dish, pie plate, or even a large loaf pan
2. **Layer 1**: Pack stuffing firmly into the bottom (~1-2 inches)
3. **Layer 2**: Add turkey pieces evenly across the stuffing
4. **Layer 3**: Distribute Brussels sprouts and other sides
5. **Layer 4**: Pour gravy liberally over everything (don't be shy—it's the glue)
6. **Layer 5**: Top with potatoes au gratin, slightly overlapping for coverage
7. **Cover tightly** with plastic wrap or foil, refrigerate

---

## Reheating

**Oven method** (best for crispy top):
- Cover with foil, bake at 350°F for 30-40 minutes until heated through
- Remove foil, broil 2-3 minutes to re-crisp the potato top

**Microwave method** (fastest):
- Cut a portion, microwave 2-3 minutes, stir/check, continue until hot
- Won't be as crispy but still delicious

---

## Notes

- Assemble while leftovers are still relatively fresh (day after, not a week later)
- The gravy is crucial—it keeps everything moist and helps flavors meld
- Can be portioned into individual containers for grab-and-go lunches
- Freezes well if wrapped tightly (thaw overnight in fridge before reheating)
- Add extra gravy when reheating if it looks dry
- Optional: top with shredded cheese before final broil for extra richness

---

## What You'll Need

- All your Turkey Day leftovers
- Deep dish or casserole vessel
- Extra gravy (always make extra gravy)
- Patience to layer instead of just dumping everything in a container
- Homemade cranberry orange compote (keep this separate, serve alongside)

**The result**: A complete Thanksgiving meal in every bite, stored in one dish, ready whenever you want it. No more Tetris-ing five containers in the microwave. The cranberry compote stays fresh in its own jar and adds that bright, tart contrast when serving.
